Variants: Rewrite and interim change = stages 4–9 (7.6, 7.7.9). Administrative change = no coordination; 3-Ltr/L3 approves (7.8.10). GM at wing level = local legal review (5.1.7). Periodic or special review (7.4.3, 7.4.4): OPR responds on a DAF Form 399 in at least 30 days. Certify Current = no changes: OPR and approving authority sign the 399, no coordination (7.5.1); the PFM marks "Certified Current on" plus the date (7.5.2). Requires Revision, Requires Transfer, Is Obsolete: the AO starts and completes that action within 180 days (7.4.4.3.3). No response: the PFM initiates a rescind action (7.4.4.4). Prescribed forms are reviewed with their publication and are not re-dated; the 399 joins the record set (8.11.3.2).

## Endorsement responses (Endorsements.Status choices)

Concur; Concur With Comment; Concur—No Response (good-faith effort recorded on 673); Nonconcur; No Comment. Ref 5.4.

## CRM columns (T-1)

Item number, Source, Type of comment (Administrative / Substantive / Critical; Critical = automatic nonconcur), Page, Paragraph, Line, Comment, Rationale, Decision. Ref 5.3.

## Mandatory coordinators

Decided (PFM, 2026-11-12, Q6): the PFM keeps the mandatory coordinators in PFM Offices: JAG (legal, last), CIO and PFM. The AO, with the PCM, adds offices with functional interest (5.1.3). The OPR identifies its own certifying official (5.6.3) and approving official (5.7).

Tables A3.1 / A3.2 not yet extracted. Records manager coordinates during formal coordination (7.1.3).

## System boundary

ANG wing (PFM, 2026-11-10): publications post to the wing's own site (1.1.3.2, 6.4), not e-Publishing through AFIMPT. 90-161.Z tracks through posting and keeps the PI (6.4.2).
