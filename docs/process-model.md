# Process model: 90-161.Z (wing/installation)

Source: DAFMAN 90-161, 18 Oct 2023, Change 1, Corrective Action 1 May 2026. Status: **draft, not verified** (extracted through a web summary). Verify each reference before the `Stages` list is built.

## Stages (one publication action)

| # | Stage | Ref | Exit condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Preparation: directive or not, existing pubs, prior record set, contact PCM/PFM | 3.1 | DAF Form 673 started |
| 2 | Drafting with SMEs | 3.2, 4.2.2.1 | Draft meets format rules (draft mode) |
| 3 | Pre-coordination (optional) | 3.3 | — |
| 4 | Formal coordination: package = Word draft (flush left) + CRM + DAF Form 673 | 3.4, 5.1, 5.1.5 | All coordinators responded; all nonconcurs resolved (5.5.1) |
| 5 | Legal coordination (servicing legal office below HQ DAF), after all other coordination and adjudication | 5.1.8.2, 3.4 | Legal concur |
| 6 | Certification: certifying official endorses DAF Form 673 | 5.6 | Endorsed |
| 7 | Approval: approving official endorses DAF Form 673; delegation letter on file | 5.7 | Endorsed |
| 8 | Submission: OPR sends 673 (or DAF Form 399 for certify-current), draft, figure files to PFM; PFM submits | 6.6.1–6.6.2 | Submitted |
| 9 | Published | — | On e-Publishing |

Variants: Rewrite and interim change = stages 4–8 (7.6, 7.7.9). Administrative change = no coordination; 3-Ltr/L3 approves (7.8.10). GM at wing level = local legal review (5.1.7). Periodic review: OPR responds in 30+ days; actions complete within 180 days (7.4.4).

## Timing

| Item | Value | Ref |
|---|---|---|
| Coordinator suspense | 15 business days (T-3) | 5.2 |
| ARC applicability review below MAJCOM/FLDCOM | at least 30 calendar days | 5.2.2 |

## Endorsement responses (Endorsements.Status choices)

Concur; Concur With Comment; Concur—No Response (good-faith effort recorded on 673); Nonconcur; No Comment. Ref 5.4.

## CRM columns (T-1)

Item number, Source, Type of comment (Administrative / Substantive / Critical; Critical = automatic nonconcur), Page, Paragraph, Line, Comment, Rationale, Decision. Ref 5.3.

## Mandatory coordinators

Tables A3.1 / A3.2 not yet extracted. Records manager coordinates during formal coordination (7.1.3).

## System boundary

Final submission to e-Publishing goes through AFIMPT per 6.6 (T-1). 90-161.Z tracks up to and including that step; it does not replace it.
