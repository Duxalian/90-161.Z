# Customer guidance, one step at a time (draft for PFM content approval)

Status: draft r3 by Claude, 12 Nov 26, 75 screens. r2 rewrote the text for the audience (PFM 3A): military members, often E5-E7, who may never have done this before and face a long, tedious process. r3 applies the second independent review (https://claude.ai/artifact/VNBoH8i4SNy7do6py58YNM, findings 2-8, 16-18, 20-23) and PFM decisions 2A, 4A and 5A (12 Nov): who must follow it and who signs, form screens, Change OPR, review requests, screens for the CSS and the signers, more words explained, shorter screens. Not approved; the PFM reviews it in the editable Claude doc https://claude.ai/artifact/8qUeXnJNPhyEJNUYU6ShzV, then the approved text comes back here. Each row becomes one PFM Guidance row (decision 12A): Guidance Key, Audience, Screen Order, Heading (Title), Instructions, DAFMAN Reference, Done When; Applies To and Process Step set the path (list design). The app shows one row per screen and moves on only when "Done When" is met.
Source: DAFMAN 90-161 (ref/dafman90-161.txt) and docs/process-model.md (stages 0-12, "New publication request" N1-N11, "Special review out of cycle" S1-S7).

Writing rules used in this draft (for the reader):
- One screen = one task. The first line says what to do; "Why" gives one reason; the app shows "Step X of Y" and the due date above it.
- Short sentences (under 25 words), "you" for the reader, no unexplained terms: every form number and term links to the "Words You Will See" panel.
- Each screen says who acts. When it is someone else's turn, the screen says so and that the app e-mails the reader when the turn comes back.
- Times are DAFMAN planning goals (Table A5.2), shown so the reader can plan, never as a threat (PFM 2026-11-11: measured tone).
- One word per role: "coordinator" for an office that reviews the draft (DAFMAN 5.1).

Audience values: AO (action officer, the customer), Any Member, Coordinator, CSS, Certifying Official, Approving Official, PFM. Squadron and flight levels are not named in DAFMAN; that text needs the PFM's approval (PFM 4A).

| REV-01 | AO | 1 | Review Task: Is Your Publication Still Right? | The PFM asks your office to check a publication: every 4 years, or sooner when a commander asks or something changed. You have at least 30 days. 1) Read it and its forms. 2) Answer on a DAF Form 399: Certify Current, Requires Revision, Requires Transfer, Is Obsolete or Other. 3) Upload it signed. Revision, transfer or obsolete: start and finish that action within 180 days; the app takes you there. If no one answers by the due date, the PFM starts removing the publication. | 7.4.2-7.4.4, 7.4.4.3.3, 7.4.4.4, 8.11.3.2; PFM 5A (12 Nov) | Signed 399 uploaded |
