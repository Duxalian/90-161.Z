# Customer guidance, one step at a time (draft for PFM content approval)

Status: draft r2 by Claude, 12 Nov 26, rewritten for the audience (PFM 3A): military members who may never have done this before and face a long, tedious process. Not approved; the PFM reviews it in the editable Claude doc https://claude.ai/artifact/8qUeXnJNPhyEJNUYU6ShzV, then the approved text comes back here. Each row becomes one PFM Guidance row (decision 12A): Guidance Key, Audience, Screen Order, Heading (Title), Instructions, DAFMAN Reference, Done When. The app shows one row per screen and moves on only when "Done When" is met.
Source: DAFMAN 90-161 (ref/dafman90-161.txt) and docs/process-model.md (stages 0-12, "New publication request" N1-N11, "Special review out of cycle" S1-S7).

Writing rules used in this draft (for the reader):
- One screen = one task. The first line says what to do; "Why" gives one reason; the app shows "Step X of Y" and the due date above it.
- Short sentences, "you" for the reader, no unexplained terms: every form number and term links to the "Words You Will See" panel.
- Each screen says who acts. When it is someone else's turn, the screen says so and that the app e-mails the reader when the turn comes back.
- Times are DAFMAN goals (Table A5.2), shown so the reader can plan, never as a threat (PFM 2026-11-11: measured tone).

Audience values: AO (action officer, the customer), Endorser, Certifying Official, Approving Official, PFM.
