# Table A3.2 local offices: seed for PFM Offices (PFM 3B, 12 Nov 26)

Source: DAFMAN 90-161 Table A3.2 "Discretionary Formal Coordinators", column "Consider Coordinating Command or Field Pubs and Forms with local:" (ref/dafman90-161.txt lines 5849-6558). Extracted by Claude on 12 Nov 26 (decision 3B). The trigger texts are DAFMAN's own words, which complete "If the publication or form: ...".
Use at build: one PFM Offices row per seed row. Office = the "Local office" text. Coordination Role = Functional. Mandatory Coordinator = No. Suggest When = the triggers, one per line. Active = Yes. The PFM adds the endorser on the site, renames the office to the wing's own name, and sets Active = No for offices the wing does not have. The seed holds only DAFMAN text: no wing names, office symbols or people.
Not seeded: items whose local column is "N/A*" (not issued at the lower level, note 1): 14 SAF/AA, 21 SAF/IE, 28 SF/CHCO, 30 SF/COO, 31 SF/CSRO, 32 SF/S6, 34 AF/TE, 35 TF-HRM. Item 13 (LeMay: "Publication OPR conducts review", terminology review) is a check for the OPR, not an office. Item 17 (local "SAF/CN, Comm Squadron") and item 19 (local "Legal") already have mandatory rows (CIO and JAG, Q13); their triggers are listed under "Mandatory rows" below for the app's help text.
Note 2 of the table: the AO ensures the unit privacy monitor reviews the publication before formal coordination (stage Privacy Review).
